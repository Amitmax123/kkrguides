What to see in Kurukshetra

Discover key pilgrimage spots, ancient temples, state-of-the-art museums, and cultural experiences.

Sacred Sites

  1. 1. Jyotisar — The sacred site where Lord Krishna narrated the Bhagavad Gita.
  2. 2. Bhishma Kund — The Mahabharata-associated site linked to Bhishma Pitamah.
  3. 3. Brahma Sarovar — One of Asia’s largest sacred water tanks.
  4. 4. Sannihit Sarovar — Believed to be the confluence of sacred waters.
  5. 5. Bhadrakali Shakti Peeth — A revered temple dedicated to Maa Bhadrakali.
  6. 6. Sthaneshwar Mahadev Temple — An ancient Shiva temple in Kurukshetra.
  7. 7. Kaleshwar Mahadev Temple — A distinctive Shiva temple.
  8. 8. Nabhkamal Temple — A sacred temple dedicated to Lord Brahma.
  9. 9. ISKCON Temple — A devotional centre dedicated to Lord Krishna.
  10. 10. Tirupati Balaji Temple — A temple inspired by the Tirupati tradition.
  11. 11. Akshardham Temple — A temple attraction inspired by Akshardham architecture.
  12. 12. Gyan Mandir — A distinctive multi-storey temple.
  13. 13. GIEO Gita Dham — A spiritual attraction promoting Bhagavad Gita teachings.

Museums & Heritage Attractions

  1. 14. Shri Krishna Museum — Exhibits dedicated to Lord Krishna and his legacy.
  2. 15. Kurukshetra Panorama & Science Centre — Mahabharata panorama and interactive science exhibits.
  3. 16. Mahabharata Anubhav Kendra — An immersive Mahabharata experience.
  4. 17. Sheikh Chilli’s Tomb — A historic Mughal-era monument and tomb complex.
  5. 18. Dharohar Haryana Museum — Showcases Haryana’s rich cultural heritage.
  6. 19. Kurukshetra University — A prominent educational institution.

Spiritual Experiences & Activities

  1. 20. Pitra Puja — Rituals performed in remembrance of ancestors.
  2. 21. Air Safari — An aerial sightseeing experience, subject to availability.
  3. 22. Brahma Sarovar Evening Aarti — A beautiful spiritual experience by the sacred Sarovar.
  4. 23. Jyotisar Light & Sound Show — An engaging presentation of Mahabharata stories.
